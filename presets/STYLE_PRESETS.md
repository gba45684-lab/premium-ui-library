# Style preset catalog

Choose one primary preset and no more than one supporting motif. Adapt every preset to the product brand, user needs, platform and technical stack.

## Glassmorphism
Frosted translucent surfaces, subtle borders, blur and layered depth. Best for selective overlays, navigation, dialogs and hero cards. Keep content readable.

## Liquid Glass-inspired
Fluid translucent layers, highlights and restrained depth. Create an original, platform-appropriate interpretation; do not copy proprietary assets or imply official affiliation.

## Bento Grid
Modular cards of varied sizes with consistent grid rhythm and responsive reflow. Useful for dashboards, summaries and feature discovery.

## Premium Minimalism
Restrained palette, precise typography, generous but efficient whitespace, subtle borders and clear alignment. Ideal for finance, productivity, resume and document apps.

## Neumorphism / Soft UI
Soft inset/raised surfaces and gentle shadows. Use sparingly and maintain contrast and clear affordances.

## Aurora / Gradient
Atmospheric gradients for focal areas and brand moments. Avoid low-contrast text or gradients on every component.

## Material Design 3
Adaptive controls, tonal color, meaningful states and familiar platform patterns. A strong Android-first option.

## Dark Luxury
Deep neutral surfaces, controlled accent color, confident typography and restrained highlights.

## Neobrutalism / Modern Brutalism
Bold typography, strong borders, high contrast and deliberate asymmetry where brand-appropriate.

## Claymorphism
Friendly rounded surfaces and soft dimensional forms for approachable consumer products.

## Editorial / Swiss
Grid-led composition, typographic hierarchy, alignment and intentional whitespace for content-rich experiences.

## Neon / Cyberpunk
Dark surfaces and bright luminous accents. Use carefully to protect readability and avoid distracting glow.

## 3D / Depth-inspired
Layering and perspective cues only where they aid comprehension. Consider device performance and reduced-motion settings.

## Product-oriented defaults
- Finance and professional tools: Premium Minimalism or Material Design 3.
- Resume and document tools: Premium Minimalism with restrained Bento Grid.
- Dashboards: Bento Grid.
- Brand-forward landing pages: selective Aurora or Glassmorphism.
- Playful consumer apps: Claymorphism.
Always inspect the existing app before deciding.
